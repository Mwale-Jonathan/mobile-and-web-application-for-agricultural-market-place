# Business Understanding

### **Problem Statement**

Agricultural producers may have difficulty making informed selling decisions because market prices vary over time and information about historical and expected prices may not be easily accessible.

### **Objectives and Goals**

- **Business Goal:**
  Create a model to project prices for volatile commodities like maize grain/mealie-meal, soybeans, or wheat. Users (e.g., smallholder farmers, milling corporations, or the Food Reserve Agency (FRA)) can use this to optimize sell timing or secure price stabilization.
- **Broad Goal:** Build a time-series or regression system that predicts the average market price (ZMW per 50kg bag) 1 to 3 months in advance with a Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) of under 10%.

### **Success Criteria:**

The project is considered successful if:

- Predict prices **1–3 months ahead**.
- Achieve **MAPE below 10%**.
- Keep prediction errors within an **acceptable ZMW range (MAE)**.
